## When UXtweak is the better choice

- You need testers recruited for you. UXtweak's User Panel does that on its paid plans. Pairpoll has no panel.
- You want to know why people chose a design. UXtweak can ask follow-up questions. Pairpoll only records the picks.
- You need more than a preference test: card sorting, tree testing, first click tests, prototype tests or session recordings.

## When Pairpoll is the better choice

- You have more than 6 designs to compare, up to 16.
- You want to compare videos on a free plan. Pairpoll can also make each video play once per pair, like a first viewing.
- You want more than a few responses without paying. A Pairpoll poll takes up to 200 voters.
- You want each voter's own top list as well as the overall ranking, and you'd like an AI agent to create the poll and read the results.

## How the test differs

In Pairpoll each voter sees two designs at a time and picks one, again and again, until their top 1, 3 or 5 is settled. Voters don't sign up: they open the link, type a name and start. Pairpoll shows which design people prefer but not why, so talk to a few voters afterwards if you need the reasons.
