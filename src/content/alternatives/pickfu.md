## When PickFu is the better choice

You need answers from strangers who match a target audience, and you don't have that audience yourself. PickFu's paid panel does this. Pairpoll has no panel: only the people you send the link to can vote.

## When Pairpoll is the better choice

- Your own viewers, customers or team should decide.
- You have more than 8 options, or you want to compare many video cuts against each other.
- You want each voter's own top list as well as the overall result.
- You want an AI agent to create the poll and read the results.

## How the voting differs

PickFu's head to head poll asks about two options at once, and its ranked poll asks people to order up to 8. In Pairpoll each voter sees two items at a time and picks one, over and over, until their top list is settled. [How voting in pairs works](/guides/how-voting-in-pairs-works) explains the details.
