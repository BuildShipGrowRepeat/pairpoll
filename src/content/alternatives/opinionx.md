## When OpinionX is the better choice

- Your options are text, such as feature ideas, problems or priorities. Pair Rank handles text and images. Pairpoll takes only images or videos.
- You have more than 16 options. OpinionX sets no limit on Pair Rank options.
- You run larger research: segmenting results by customer group, MaxDiff, conjoint or pricing studies.

[MaxDiff vs ranking questions vs pairwise comparison](/guides/maxdiff-vs-pairwise-comparison) explains which method fits which kind of list.

## When Pairpoll is the better choice

- You're comparing videos, with the option that each video plays once per pair before the voter picks.
- You want every voter to finish with a settled top 1, 3 or 5, not only a share of wins.
- You want it free for your own audience, and you'd like an AI agent to create the poll and read the results through the API, the command line tool or the MCP server.

## How the ranking differs

A win rate counts how often each option won the pairs it appeared in. In Pairpoll each voter runs a short knockout: the option they pick moves on, until their top list is settled. The overall ranking then gives points for each place in each finished list. [How voting in pairs works](/guides/how-voting-in-pairs-works) explains it step by step.
