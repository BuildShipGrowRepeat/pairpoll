## When PollUnit is the better choice

- You need other kinds of voting as well, such as range voting, jury voting or a photo or video contest.
- Your options are text, such as names or ideas. PollUnit's pairwise comparison takes text or images. Pairpoll takes only images or videos.
- You want each person to vote on a set number of random pairs instead of finishing a full knockout.

## When Pairpoll is the better choice

- You want to compare videos head to head. Pairpoll can play each video once per pair, with no replay, pause or seeking, before the voter picks.
- You want each voter's own top list as well as the combined ranking.
- You want an AI agent to create the poll and read the results from a command line tool, as well as through an MCP server.
- You want it free, without a plan to choose.

## How the ranking differs

With random pairs, each person sees a sample of pairs and the ranking comes from everyone's picks together. An ELO rating moves an option up when it wins a pair and down when it loses, with bigger moves for surprising results.

In Pairpoll each voter finishes a short knockout, so every voter ends with a complete top 1, 3 or 5. The overall ranking gives points for each place in each finished list. [How voting in pairs works](/guides/how-voting-in-pairs-works) explains the details, including what a knockout can't tell you.
