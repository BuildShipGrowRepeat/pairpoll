## When StrawPoll is the better choice

- Your options are text: a yes or no question, names, dates or ideas. Pairpoll takes only images or videos.
- You need to find a meeting time. StrawPoll has a meeting poll for that.
- You want the poll embedded on your own website. Pairpoll has no embed: voters open a link.
- You expect more than 200 voters. That is the limit for one Pairpoll poll.

## When Pairpoll is the better choice

- Your options are pictures or videos, and the differences between them are small. Side by side, two at a time, small differences are easier to see.
- You're comparing videos. Pairpoll takes MP4 or WebM files and can play each video once per pair, with no replay, pause or seeking.
- You have more than a handful of options, up to 16. Choosing one from a grid of twelve is hard. Choosing one of two is quick.
- You want each voter's own top 1, 3 or 5 as well as the overall ranking.
- You want an AI agent to create the poll and read the results.

## How the voting differs

A classic poll shows every option at once and counts the votes for each. In Pairpoll a voter never sees the whole list. They see two items and pick one. Items run through a knockout: the ones they pick move on to later rounds until their top list is settled. The overall ranking gives points for each place in every finished voter's list. [How voting in pairs works](/guides/how-voting-in-pairs-works) explains it step by step, and [the pairwise ranking page](/for/pairwise-ranking) shows how many picks a voter makes.

For a set of photos, [let people pick the best photo](/for/photos) shows how a poll works.

## Common questions

### Is Pairpoll free?

Yes. Pairpoll is free and open source, with no ads. Polls, files and votes are deleted 30 days after a poll is created.

### Do voters need an account?

No. Voters open the link, type a name and start. Only the person who creates the poll signs in, with an email link.

### Can I embed a Pairpoll poll on my website?

No. You share a link. To show the outcome to others, you can turn on a read-only results link.

### Can I mix images and videos in one poll?

No. One poll holds 2 to 16 images or 2 to 16 videos.
