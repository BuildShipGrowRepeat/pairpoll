## When Lyssna is the better choice

- You need participants recruited for you. Lyssna has a participant panel, priced separately from its plans. Pairpoll has no panel.
- You need more than a preference test: usability tests, card sorting, first click tests, surveys or interviews.
- Your team already runs its user research in one platform and wants this test there too.

## When Pairpoll is the better choice

- You have more than 6 designs to compare, up to 16.
- You're comparing videos and want each one to play once per pair, like a first viewing.
- You want each voter's own top list as well as the overall ranking.
- You want it free for your own users, customers or team, and you'd like an AI agent to create the poll and read the results.

## How the test differs

In Pairpoll each voter sees two items at a time and picks one, again and again, until their top list is settled. Voters don't sign up: they open the link, type a name and start. Pairpoll doesn't ask follow-up questions, so it shows which design people prefer but not why. If you need the reasons, a research platform like Lyssna fits better.
