## When this helps

You have three or four directions for a new pack, or a redesign of one that already sells. Printing is expensive and hard to undo, so you want to hear from shoppers before the files go to the printer. Research firms run packaging studies with paid panels. If you have customers, followers or a mailing list, you can ask them yourself for free.

## How a poll for packaging works

1. Export a render or photo of each design at the same size, angle and light.
2. Create a poll with a shopper's question, such as "Which one would you pick up?"
3. Choose a top 1 to find the winner, or a top 3 to see the runner-up directions too.
4. Send the link to your customers, newsletter readers or retail contacts. Voters type a name and start. They don't sign up.
5. Open the results. You see the overall ranking and each voter's top list.

## Show packs the way shoppers meet them

On a shelf or a product page, a shopper sees the front of the pack, small, next to other products. Make the poll look like that moment.

- Use the front face, at about the size a shopper sees it.
- Keep the angle, light and background the same for every version.
- Change one thing between versions if you can: the colour, the main image or the product name's size. Then you learn which change people liked.

## What it doesn't do

Pairpoll asks people which design they prefer. It doesn't simulate a shelf, track where people look or measure recall or purchase intent, and it has no panel of shoppers. If you need answers from strangers in a target market, or a study you can show a retailer, a research firm with a consumer panel fits better.

## Tips

- Include the current pack as one option. It tells you whether a new design is preferred at all.
- Ask buyers, not the team. You know what's inside the box. A shopper only knows what the front says.
- Check the print rules first: legal text, barcode space and the dieline. There's no point voting on a design you can't print.

## Common questions

### How many people should vote?

A small group of the right people is enough to drop the weak designs. To separate two close favourites you need many more. [How many voters do you need?](/guides/how-many-voters-do-you-need) shows how to read small numbers.

### Can a buyer at a retailer vote without an account?

Yes. Voters open the link, type a name and start. Only the person who creates the poll signs in.

### Is this an A/B test?

No. A poll measures what people prefer when they look on purpose. [Preference testing vs A/B testing](/guides/preference-testing-vs-ab-testing) explains the difference.

### Can an AI agent set up the poll?

Yes. An agent can create the poll from a folder of renders with the command line tool, or through the API or MCP server. See the [agent docs](/docs/agents).
