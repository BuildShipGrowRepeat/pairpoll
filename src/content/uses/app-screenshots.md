## When this helps

You're preparing a store page or an update, and you have several options for the first screenshot: a caption above the phone, one big feature, a dark version, a plain screen. You can only ship one set. Asking the people who use your app, or would, is quicker than guessing and doesn't need a live store experiment.

## How a poll for app screenshots works

1. Export each option as an image at its real size. Tall screenshots are shown whole, so voters see the full frame.
2. Create a poll with a question such as "Which screenshot makes you want to try the app?"
3. Choose a top size. A top 1 settles the first screenshot. A top 3 or 5 gives you a running order for the rest.
4. Send the link to beta testers, a mailing list or a community. Voters type a name and start, without signing up.
5. Open the results. You see the overall ranking and each voter's top list.

## Comparing whole sets

Pairpoll compares single images. To compare complete screenshot sets, put each set side by side in one wide image, the way the store shows them, and upload one image per set. Voters then pick between sets.

## Pairpoll and the stores' own tests

Both stores can test listing graphics on live traffic. Apple's [product page optimization](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/product-page-optimization/) tests up to three alternate versions of icons, screenshots and app previews with a share of App Store visitors. Google Play's [store listing experiments](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/6227309) test icons, feature graphics and screenshots with a share of listing visitors.

Those tests need a published app and traffic. Pairpoll is for before that: narrow many options down to the two or three worth a store test, or choose for a new app that has no traffic yet.

## Common questions

### Who should vote?

People who would download this kind of app. Your beta testers and current users are a good start. Your team knows every screen already, so their picks tell you less.

### Can I test app preview videos?

Yes, in a separate video poll. Export each preview as MP4 or WebM and turn on "each video plays once" if you want a first-viewing reaction.

### Can my AI agent set up the poll from a folder of screenshots?

Yes. The `pairpoll` command line tool creates a poll from a folder of files in one step, and the API and MCP server can create polls and read results too. See the [agent docs](/docs/agents).

### Do voters need the app?

No. Voters open a web link on any phone or computer.
