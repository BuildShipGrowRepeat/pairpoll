## When this helps

You've narrowed a shoot down to a dozen good frames and can't choose between them. Maybe it's the photo for a holiday card, a print for the wall, a headshot for a speaker page, the cover of an album or the shots for your portfolio. You've looked at them too long to see them fresh. Other people haven't.

## How a photo poll works

1. Cull first. Pairpoll takes up to 16 photos in a poll, and every extra photo adds picks for each voter.
2. Create a poll with a question that says what the photo is for, such as "Which one should go on the wall?" or "Which headshot looks most like someone you'd hire?"
3. Choose a top 1, 3 or 5. For a portfolio or a set of prints, a top 5 gives you a shortlist from every voter.
4. Send the link in a group chat, by email or wherever your people are. Voters type a name and start, with no sign-up.
5. Open the results to see the overall ranking and each person's own top list.

Pairpoll shows each photo whole, without cropping, so voters judge the frame you actually took. The order is shuffled for every voter, so no photo gets an advantage from coming first.

## Tips

- Group similar shots. If you have five near-identical frames of one moment, pick the sharpest yourself and put only that one in the poll.
- Keep edits consistent. If one photo is graded and the others aren't, people may be voting on the edit rather than the shot.
- Ask people who know the purpose. A client who will print the photo judges differently from a friend scrolling on a phone.

## Common questions

### Are my photos public?

No. Poll links are unlisted and hidden from search engines. Anyone with the link can open the poll, so share it only with people you trust. Photos, votes and the poll are deleted 30 days after the poll is created.

### Do voters see each other's votes?

No. Each voter sees their own top list at the end. You see everyone's lists and the overall ranking. If you want to show the result, you can turn on a read-only results link, which hides voter names unless you choose to show them.

### Which image files work?

JPEG, PNG, WebP and GIF. One poll holds either images or videos.

### Can I see who picked which photo?

Yes. You see each voter's name, the time and their top list, and you can delete any entry.
