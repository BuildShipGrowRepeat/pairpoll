## How it works for thumbnails

1. Export 2 to 16 thumbnail options as images.
2. Create a poll. Write the question voters will see, for example "Which video would you click?", and choose whether each person settles a top 1, 3 or 5.
3. Share the link where your viewers already are: your community tab, a Discord server, a newsletter or a group chat.
4. Open the results. You see each voter's top list and an overall ranking.

## Why pairs suit thumbnails

On YouTube, a viewer sees your thumbnail next to other videos and makes a quick choice. Picking one of two is close to that moment, and it's easier than giving each image a score.

## Real people instead of an AI score

Many thumbnail raters give each image a score from a model. Pairpoll shows your options to the people you choose, so the ranking comes from your own viewers.

## What it doesn't do

Pairpoll doesn't test thumbnails on live YouTube traffic. YouTube's own [A/B test for titles and thumbnails](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13861714) does that, in YouTube Studio for your own videos. Use Pairpoll before you publish, to narrow a long list down to the two or three worth testing.

## Tips

- Ask the question your viewer would ask. "Which would you click?" works better than "Which is best?"
- If you want to learn what works, change one thing between versions, such as the face, the colour or the text.
- Ask people who watch your kind of videos. Friends who don't watch them will judge by different rules.
