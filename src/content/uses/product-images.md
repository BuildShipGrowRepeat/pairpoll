## When this helps

You sell on a marketplace or your own shop, and you have several candidates for the main photo: a clean shot on white, the product in use, a close-up of the detail that sets it apart. The main photo is the one shoppers see first in a list of results, so it's worth asking the people who buy from you which one they'd click.

## How a poll for product images works

1. Export each option at the same size and ratio your shop uses.
2. Create a poll with a shopper's question, such as "Which photo would make you open this listing?"
3. Choose a top 1 if you only need the main photo, or a top 3 or 5 if you're also choosing the photos that follow it.
4. Send the link to customers: a newsletter, a post-purchase email, or your followers. Voters type a name and start. They don't sign up.
5. Open the results. You see the overall ranking and each voter's top list.

The same poll works for packaging designs, colour options and the order of photos in a gallery.

## Tips

- Show each option the way shoppers will see it. If the photo appears small in search results, a small version in the poll is a fairer test.
- Keep one thing different at a time: the background, the angle or a prop. Then you learn what to reuse for the next product.
- Ask buyers, not makers. You and your supplier know what the product is. A shopper only knows what the photo shows.
- Check the marketplace's own image rules before you poll. There's no point voting on a photo you can't use.

## What a poll can't tell you

A poll shows which photo people prefer when asked. It doesn't measure clicks or sales on the marketplace. Use it to pick a strong main photo before you list. If your shop can test images on live traffic, run that afterwards with the top two.

## Common questions

### Who should vote on product photos?

People who buy, or would buy, that kind of product. Your own customers are a good start. If you don't have customers yet and need answers from strangers in a target group, a paid panel such as [PickFu](/alternatives/pickfu) fits better than Pairpoll.

### How many photos can I compare?

From 2 to 16 in one poll.

### Can I get the results into a spreadsheet?

Yes. You can export the results as CSV or JSON.

### Is it really free?

Yes. Pairpoll is free and open source. Polls, files and votes are deleted 30 days after a poll is created.
