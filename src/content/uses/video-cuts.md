## When this helps

You've edited a trailer, an ad, a YouTube intro or a short three ways. One opens on the problem, one on the product, one on a face. You've watched them so often that you can't see them the way a first-time viewer does. A poll lets other people watch them fresh and tells you which cut they would keep watching.

## Why "plays once" matters

When you turn on "each video plays once", both videos in a pair play to the end before the vote buttons appear. Voters can't replay, pause or skip ahead. That's close to how people meet your video for real: they see it once and decide. Without it, a voter can rewatch the end of one cut and judge details nobody notices on a first viewing.

Videos start when the voter taps and play with sound available, and the voter page works on phones.

## How a poll for video cuts works

1. Export each cut as MP4 or WebM.
2. Create a poll and write the question, such as "Which version would you watch to the end?"
3. Turn on "each video plays once" and choose a top size.
4. Send the link to people who watch this kind of video: your subscribers, a community or a few colleagues who haven't seen the edits.
5. Open the results to see the overall ranking and each viewer's top list.

## Keep the watching time short

With "plays once" on, each pick means watching two videos to the end. A voter needs one pick fewer than the number of cuts to find their winner, plus a few more for the places after first. Four one-minute cuts with a top 1 means three pairs, so about six minutes of watching per voter.

- Test the part that differs. If only the first 15 seconds change, export just the openings.
- Compare fewer cuts. Drop the ones you already know won't ship.
- Use a top 1 for long videos. A top 3 adds more pairs to watch.

## Common questions

### Can voters skip or rewatch?

With "plays once" on, no. Each video in a pair plays once, start to finish. With it off, voters can play, pause and seek freely.

### What happens if a video doesn't load?

The voter gets a retry, and a video that failed to load doesn't count as watched.

### Can I compare an image with a video?

No. One poll holds either images or videos. To compare thumbnails for the video, run a separate [thumbnail poll](/for/thumbnails).

### What if a voter reloads the page?

They can close the page and come back in the same browser to carry on. A reload during a pair doesn't unlock the vote before both videos have played.
